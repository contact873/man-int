03
Auto parts

Parts, by system.

Mixed orders are consolidated into one container, so a dealer can restock several lines in a single shipment rather than five.

Engine and filtration
  • Oil, air, fuel and cabin filters
  • Belts, hoses and cooling components
  • Engine mounts and gasket sets
  • Lubricants and service kits

Brake and suspension

  • Pads, discs, drums and shoes
  • Shock absorbers and struts
  • Bushes, arms and ball joints
  • Wheel bearings and hub assemblies
Electrical and body
  • Batteries, alternators and starters
  • Lighting, sensors and switches
  • Body panels, mirrors and trim
  • Glass and weather seals

What we need from you

  • The part number, or the vehicle's chassis number
  • The quantity you actually need, not an estimate
  • The destination port
  • Whether the order is one-off or a repeating line

A photograph of the old part beats a description. Send it on WhatsApp and we will match it against the Japanese catalogue.

04
Process

Four steps, and the quote comes after the inspection.

STEP 1

Requirement

Tell us the model, year, budget or the part numbers, and the port. We confirm what is available before quoting.

STEP 2

Inspection and quote

Condition report, photographs and the auction sheet translated, then a written FOB or CIF price with the lead time.

STEP 3

Documents

Invoice, export certificate, bill of lading and origin papers prepared in the buyer's name.

STEP 4

Shipping

Container or ro-ro to the port you name, with the tracking and arrival documents sent as they are issued.

We do not promise a delivery date we cannot hold, and we do not quote a price that changes after the deposit. The written figure is the figure.

05
Quality and inspection

Checked before it is bought. Documented before it ships.

The same three habits on every order. That consistency is what makes a second container predictable, and it is the only quality claim worth making.

BEFORE PURCHASE

Inspection

The auction sheet is translated and the faults stated before you commit. The condition photographs are of the actual vehicle, never a stock picture of the model.

BEFORE SHIPPING

Documents

Invoice, export certificate, bill of lading and origin papers, prepared in the buyer's name and listed in advance, so nothing is discovered at the destination.

EVERY ORDER

One process

Four steps, the same each time, with the written figure held. A supplier whose process changes with every customer cannot be relied on for a container.

What we do not promise.

Nothing on this page is a promise of a specific vehicle, a price held indefinitely, or a delivery date. Used vehicles are sold as inspected, and the auction grade and condition notes are given before purchase.

Import rules, duties and registration requirements in your country are yours to confirm. We will tell you what documents we provide, and you confirm what your customs authority requires.

Prices are quoted FOB a Japanese port unless stated otherwise, and move with the exchange rate. The figure confirmed in writing at the time of order is the one that applies.
